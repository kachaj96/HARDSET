## R01 — Currier et al. (2026)

**Tytuł:** ACSM Position Stand. Resistance Training Prescription for Muscle Function, Hypertrophy, and Physical Performance in Healthy Adults: An Overview of Reviews.  
**Rodzaj:** Umbrella review / position stand.  
**DOI:** [10.1249/MSS.0000000000003897](https://doi.org/10.1249/MSS.0000000000003897).  
**Link bibliograficzny:** https://doi.org/10.1249/MSS.0000000000003897

**Uzasadniony wniosek:** Resistance training benefits muscle outcomes; ≥10 sets/muscle/week tended to enhance hypertrophy; heavy loads enhance 1RM; failure and periodization not consistently superior.

**Granice interpretacji:** Overview of prior reviews; group tendencies, not personalized thresholds; coding of sets varies.

## R02 — Pelland et al. (2026)

**Tytuł:** The Resistance Training Dose Response: Meta-Regressions Exploring the Effects of Weekly Volume and Frequency on Muscle Hypertrophy and Strength Gains.  
**Rodzaj:** Systematic review / meta-regression.  
**DOI:** [10.1007/s40279-025-02344-w](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-025-02344-w).  
**Link bibliograficzny:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-025-02344-w

**Uzasadniony wniosek:** Greater weekly training volume generally supports hypertrophy with diminishing returns; fractional indirect-set counting fit dataset best.

**Granice interpretacji:** Statistical fractional coefficient is NOT a universal 1S=0.5P physiological constant; correlational dose modeling.

## R03 — Grgic et al. (2022)

**Tytuł:** Effects of resistance training performed to repetition failure or non-failure on muscular strength and hypertrophy: A systematic review and meta-analysis.  
**Rodzaj:** Systematic review / meta-analysis.  
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.jshs.2021.01.007](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jshs.2021.01.007).  
**Link bibliograficzny:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jshs.2021.01.007

**Uzasadniony wniosek:** No statistically clear overall superiority of momentary failure over nonfailure for strength or hypertrophy.

**Granice interpretacji:** Study definitions, volume and populations vary; does not establish best precise RIR.

## R04 — Robinson et al. (2024)

**Tytuł:** Exploring the Dose-Response Relationship Between Estimated Resistance Training Proximity to Failure, Strength Gain, and Muscle Hypertrophy: A Series of Meta-Regressions.  
**Rodzaj:** Exploratory meta-regression.  
**DOI:** [10.1007/s40279-024-02069-2](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-024-02069-2).  
**Link bibliograficzny:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-024-02069-2

**Uzasadniony wniosek:** Hypertrophy may improve closer to failure; strength gains occur over wider RIR; exact relationship uncertain.

**Granice interpretacji:** RIR retrospectively estimated in constituent studies; not proof that 0 RIR is optimal.

## R05 — Refalo et al. (2023)

**Tytuł:** Influence of Resistance Training Proximity-to-Failure on Skeletal Muscle Hypertrophy: A Systematic Review with Meta-analysis.  
**Rodzaj:** Systematic review / meta-analysis.  
**DOI:** [10.1007/s40279-022-01784-y](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-022-01784-y).  
**Link bibliograficzny:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-022-01784-y

**Uzasadniony wniosek:** Momentary muscular failure did not clearly outperform nonfailure in subgroup analysis.

**Granice interpretacji:** Outcome depends on operational definitions and evidence base.

## R06 — Vieira et al. (2021)

**Tytuł:** Effects of Resistance Training Performed to Failure or Not to Failure on Muscle Strength, Hypertrophy, and Power Output: A Systematic Review With Meta-Analysis.  
**Rodzaj:** Systematic review / meta-analysis.  
**DOI:** [10.1519/JSC.0000000000003936](https://doi.org/10.1519/JSC.0000000000003936).  
**Link bibliograficzny:** https://doi.org/10.1519/JSC.0000000000003936

**Uzasadniony wniosek:** When volume equated, failure vs nonfailure did not clearly differ for hypertrophy; context affects apparent results.

**Granice interpretacji:** Hypertrophy subset small; cannot support exact 1/0 RIR protocol.

## R07 — Schoenfeld, Ogborn & Krieger (2017)

**Tytuł:** Dose-response relationship between weekly resistance training volume and increases in muscle mass: A systematic review and meta-analysis.  
**Rodzaj:** Systematic review / meta-analysis.  
**DOI:** [10.1080/02640414.2016.1210197](https://doi.org/10.1080/02640414.2016.1210197).  
**Link bibliograficzny:** https://doi.org/10.1080/02640414.2016.1210197

**Uzasadniony wniosek:** On average greater weekly set volume linked to greater hypertrophy.

**Granice interpretacji:** Older relatively small literature; per-muscle set counting differs across studies.

## R08 — Schoenfeld et al. (2019)

**Tytuł:** Resistance Training Volume Enhances Muscle Hypertrophy but Not Strength in Trained Men.  
**Rodzaj:** Randomized controlled trial.  
**DOI:** [10.1249/MSS.0000000000001764](https://doi.org/10.1249/MSS.0000000000001764).  
**Link bibliograficzny:** https://doi.org/10.1249/MSS.0000000000001764

**Uzasadniony wniosek:** Higher volumes may provide additional size gains in trained men, not necessarily matching incremental strength gains.

**Granice interpretacji:** Specific sample, length and program; cannot be generalized as universally best volume.

## R09 — Carvalho et al. (2022)

**Tytuł:** Muscle hypertrophy and strength gains after resistance training with different volume-matched loads: a systematic review and meta-analysis.  
**Rodzaj:** Systematic review / meta-analysis.  
**DOI:** [10.1139/apnm-2021-0515](https://doi.org/10.1139/apnm-2021-0515).  
**Link bibliograficzny:** https://doi.org/10.1139/apnm-2021-0515

**Uzasadniony wniosek:** Heavier training generally favored maximum strength; hypertrophy outcomes more similar across load ranges.

**Granice interpretacji:** Volume-load equivalence not identical to weekly set matching; inclusion protocols vary.

## R10 — Schoenfeld, Grgic & Krieger (2019)

**Tytuł:** How many times per week should a muscle be trained to maximize muscle hypertrophy? A systematic review and meta-analysis of studies examining the effects of resistance training frequency.  
**Rodzaj:** Systematic review / meta-analysis.  
**DOI:** [10.1080/02640414.2018.1555906](https://doi.org/10.1080/02640414.2018.1555906).  
**Link bibliograficzny:** https://doi.org/10.1080/02640414.2018.1555906

**Uzasadniony wniosek:** Volume-equated hypertrophy effects largely similar across different weekly frequencies.

**Granice interpretacji:** HARDSET 2-exposure rule is organizational, not a biological must.

## R11 — Ramos-Campo et al. (2024)

**Tytuł:** Efficacy of Split Versus Full-Body Resistance Training on Strength and Muscle Growth: A Systematic Review With Meta-Analysis.  
**Rodzaj:** Systematic review / meta-analysis.  
**DOI:** [10.1519/JSC.0000000000004774](https://doi.org/10.1519/JSC.0000000000004774).  
**Link bibliograficzny:** https://doi.org/10.1519/JSC.0000000000004774

**Uzasadniony wniosek:** When volume equated, split vs full-body yielded similar hypertrophy and strength outcomes in reviewed studies.

**Granice interpretacji:** Small/moderate evidence base; individual schedules and fatigue still matter.

## R12 — Haugen et al. (2023)

**Tytuł:** Effect of free-weight vs. machine-based strength training on maximal strength, hypertrophy and jump performance – a systematic review and meta-analysis.  
**Rodzaj:** Systematic review / meta-analysis.  
**DOI:** [10.1186/s13102-023-00713-4](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13102-023-00713-4).  
**Link bibliograficzny:** https://doi.org/10.1186/s13102-023-00713-4

**Uzasadniony wniosek:** No consistent hypertrophy advantage for machines or free weights; strength has task specificity.

**Granice interpretacji:** Only subset measured hypertrophy; selection should account for skill/safety.

## R13 — Moesgaard et al. (2022)

**Tytuł:** Effects of Periodization on Strength and Muscle Hypertrophy in Volume-Equated Resistance Training Programs: A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis.  
**Rodzaj:** Systematic review / meta-analysis.  
**DOI:** [10.1007/s40279-021-01636-1](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-021-01636-1).  
**Link bibliograficzny:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-021-01636-1

**Uzasadniony wniosek:** Periodization offered strength benefits in reviewed settings; no clear hypertrophy advantage when volume equated.

**Granice interpretacji:** Volume equalization, populations and block designs differ.

## R14 — Bickel, Cross & Bamman (2011)

**Tytuł:** Exercise dosing to retain resistance training adaptations in young and older adults.  
**Rodzaj:** Longitudinal controlled exercise study.  
**DOI:** [10.1249/MSS.0b013e318207c15d](https://doi.org/10.1249/MSS.0b013e318207c15d).  
**Link bibliograficzny:** https://doi.org/10.1249/MSS.0b013e318207c15d

**Uzasadniony wniosek:** Young participants preserved many size adaptations at substantially reduced dose; older participants showed different maintenance responses.

**Granice interpretacji:** Specific detraining/maintenance scenario; no universal maintenance-minimum sets.

## R15 — Schumann et al. (2022)

**Tytuł:** Compatibility of Concurrent Aerobic and Strength Training for Skeletal Muscle Size and Function: An Updated Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis.  
**Rodzaj:** Systematic review / meta-analysis.  
**DOI:** [10.1007/s40279-021-01587-7](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-021-01587-7).  
**Link bibliograficzny:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-021-01587-7

**Uzasadniony wniosek:** Concurrent endurance and strength training did not generally compromise hypertrophy/maximal strength; explosive strength may be attenuated.

**Granice interpretacji:** Individual workload and scheduling still important; not a personal tolerance guarantee.

## R16 — Singer et al. (2024)

**Tytuł:** Give it a rest: a systematic review with Bayesian meta-analysis on the effect of inter-set rest interval duration on muscle hypertrophy.  
**Rodzaj:** Systematic review / Bayesian meta-analysis.  
**DOI:** [10.3389/fspor.2024.1429789](https://doi.org/10.3389/fspor.2024.1429789).  
**Link bibliograficzny:** https://doi.org/10.3389/fspor.2024.1429789

**Uzasadniony wniosek:** Rest interval length can influence quality of volume and possibly hypertrophy; a practical rest period should sustain output.

**Granice interpretacji:** Does not validate the precise HARDSET 2.5–4 / 1.5–3 min bands for every user.

## R17 — Schoenfeld et al. (2017)

**Tytuł:** Strength and Hypertrophy Adaptations Between Low- vs. High-Load Resistance Training: A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis.  
**Rodzaj:** Systematic review / meta-analysis.  
**DOI:** [10.1519/JSC.0000000000002200](https://doi.org/10.1519/JSC.0000000000002200).  
**Link bibliograficzny:** https://doi.org/10.1519/JSC.0000000000002200

**Uzasadniony wniosek:** Hypertrophy can develop across loading ranges; heavier loads generally favor maximal dynamic strength.

**Granice interpretacji:** Conditions often involved sets to failure; no universal best rep range.

## R18 — Varovic et al. (2025)

**Tytuł:** Does Muscle Length Influence Regional Hypertrophy? A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis.  
**Rodzaj:** Systematic review / Bayesian meta-analysis.  
**DOI:** [10.1055/a-2615-4935](https://doi.org/10.1055/a-2615-4935).  
**Link bibliograficzny:** https://doi.org/10.1055/a-2615-4935

**Uzasadniony wniosek:** Evidence about longer vs shorter mean muscle length suggests small/contextual differences; caution with sweeping claims.

**Granice interpretacji:** Studies differ in achieved muscle-length contrasts, muscles and regional measures.

## R19 — Lovegrove et al. (2022)

**Tytuł:** Repetitions in Reserve Is a Reliable Tool for Prescribing Resistance Training Load.  
**Rodzaj:** Repeated-measures reliability study.  
**DOI:** [10.1519/JSC.0000000000003952](https://doi.org/10.1519/JSC.0000000000003952).  
**Link bibliograficzny:** https://doi.org/10.1519/JSC.0000000000003952

**Uzasadniony wniosek:** RIR-based load prescription can be repeatable in tested novice participants and exercises.

**Granice interpretacji:** Reliability of load selection is not perfect accuracy of RIR on every rep/exercise.

## R20 — Mansfield et al. (2020)

**Tytuł:** Estimating Repetitions in Reserve for Resistance Exercise: An Analysis of Factors Which Impact on Prediction Accuracy.  
**Rodzaj:** Repeated-measures estimation study.  
**DOI:** [10.1519/JSC.0000000000003779](https://doi.org/10.1519/JSC.0000000000003779).  
**Link bibliograficzny:** https://doi.org/10.1519/JSC.0000000000003779

**Uzasadniony wniosek:** RIR estimates varied; estimates tended to underestimate actual reserve, and improved closer to failure in tested protocols.

**Granice interpretacji:** Small sample and limited lifts/loads; RIR remains an estimate, not objective truth.

## R21 — Marques et al. (2025)

**Tytuł:** Muscle Growth Is Very Strongly Correlated with Strength Gains after Lower Body Resistance Training: New Insight from Within-Participant Associations.  
**Rodzaj:** Longitudinal mechanistic study.  
**DOI:** [10.1249/MSS.0000000000003819](https://doi.org/10.1249/MSS.0000000000003819).  
**Link bibliograficzny:** https://doi.org/10.1249/MSS.0000000000003819

**Uzasadniony wniosek:** Within-participant correlations of size and strength gains were strong in one specific lower-body protocol.

**Granice interpretacji:** Does not make strength a precise non-imaging estimator of all-muscle hypertrophy.

## R22 — Balshaw et al. (2026)

## R24 — Balsalobre-Fernández et al. (2016)

**Tytuł:** Effects of Strength Training on Running Economy in Highly Trained Runners: A Systematic Review With Meta-Analysis of Controlled Trials.  
**Rodzaj:** Systematic review / meta-analysis.  
**DOI:** [10.1519/JSC.0000000000001316](https://doi.org/10.1519/JSC.0000000000001316).  
**Link bibliograficzny:** https://doi.org/10.1519/JSC.0000000000001316

**Uzasadniony wniosek:** Strength training (resistance plus plyometric, 2-3x/week, 8-12 weeks) improved running economy in highly trained middle- and long-distance runners (SMD -1.42).

**Granice interpretacji:** Only 5 studies, 93 runners with VO2max >60; wide confidence interval; mostly low-moderate loads combined with jumps and sprints.

## R25 — Llanos-Lagos et al. (2024)

**Tytuł:** Effect of Strength Training Programs in Middle- and Long-Distance Runners' Economy at Different Running Speeds: A Systematic Review with Meta-analysis.  
**Rodzaj:** Systematic review / three-level meta-analysis.  
**DOI:** [10.1007/s40279-023-01978-y](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-023-01978-y).  
**Link bibliograficzny:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-023-01978-y

**Uzasadniony wniosek:** High-load (>=80% 1RM), plyometric and combined strength training improved running economy (small to moderate effects); submaximal-load (40-79% 1RM) and isometric training did not.

**Granice interpretacji:** Certainty moderate (low for combined); effects depend on running speed and VO2max; runner populations only.

## R26 — Llanos-Lagos et al. (2024)

**Tytuł:** The Effect of Strength Training Methods on Middle-Distance and Long-Distance Runners' Athletic Performance: A Systematic Review with Meta-analysis.  
**Rodzaj:** Systematic review / meta-analysis.  
**DOI:** [10.1007/s40279-024-02018-z](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-024-02018-z).  
**Link bibliograficzny:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-024-02018-z

**Uzasadniony wniosek:** High-load and combined strength training improved running performance (time trial, time to exhaustion); plyometric alone not significant; no method changed VO2max, vVO2max, MMSS or sprint capacity.

**Granice interpretacji:** Certainty very low to moderate; heterogeneous protocols and populations.

## R27 — Lauersen et al. (2018)

**Tytuł:** Strength training as superior, dose-dependent and safe prevention of acute and overuse sports injuries: a systematic review, qualitative analysis and meta-analysis.  
**Rodzaj:** Systematic review / meta-analysis of RCTs.  
**DOI:** [10.1136/bjsports-2018-099078](https://doi.org/10.1136/bjsports-2018-099078).  
**Link bibliograficzny:** https://doi.org/10.1136/bjsports-2018-099078

**Uzasadniony wniosek:** Strength-training-based prevention programmes reduced sports injury risk (RR 0.338) in athletes aged 12-40, with a dose-response association.

**Granice interpretacji:** Only 6 RCTs, 177 injuries; mixed sports and ages; not specific to runners.

## R28 — Wu et al. (2024)

**Tytuł:** Do Exercise-Based Prevention Programs Reduce Injury in Endurance Runners? A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis.  
**Rodzaj:** Systematic review / meta-analysis of RCTs.  
**DOI:** [10.1007/s40279-024-01993-7](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-024-01993-7).  
**Link bibliograficzny:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-024-01993-7

**Uzasadniony wniosek:** In endurance runners, exercise-based prevention programmes did not significantly reduce running-related injury risk or rate overall; a post hoc analysis of supervised programmes only showed lower risk.

**Granice interpretacji:** 9 RCTs, 1904 participants, 7 of low quality; supervised-only finding is post hoc and may reflect compliance.

## R29 — Bohm et al. (2015)

**Tytuł:** Human tendon adaptation in response to mechanical loading: a systematic review and meta-analysis of exercise intervention studies on healthy adults.  
**Rodzaj:** Systematic review / meta-analysis.  
**DOI:** [10.1186/s40798-015-0009-9](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40798-015-0009-9).  
**Link bibliograficzny:** https://doi.org/10.1186/s40798-015-0009-9

**Uzasadniony wniosek:** Achilles and patellar tendons increased stiffness (SMD 0.70), Young's modulus and CSA after >=8-week loading interventions; stiffness adaptation depended on loading intensity, not contraction type.

**Granice interpretacji:** Healthy adults 18-50; 264 participants; tendon properties, not injury or running outcomes.

## R30 — Lazarczuk et al. (2022)

**Tytuł:** Mechanical, Material and Morphological Adaptations of Healthy Lower Limb Tendons to Mechanical Loading: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis.  
**Rodzaj:** Systematic review / meta-analysis.  
**DOI:** [10.1007/s40279-022-01695-y](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-022-01695-y).  
**Link bibliograficzny:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-022-01695-y

**Uzasadniony wniosek:** Mechanical loading, mainly resistance training, increased lower-limb tendon stiffness (SMD 0.74) and modulus; higher-strain resistance protocols produced greater adaptation.

**Granice interpretacji:** Tendon properties only; does not show reduced injury or better running performance.
