# HARDSET — Provenance, reproducibility and editorial limits

## What is and is not original verbatim text

- **Chapters 01–05:** faithful editorial reconstructions of previously **approved final rules**, prepared from retrievable chapter decisions. Their wording is **not guaranteed identical** to the historical final texts. No new scientific claims or personal preferences are intentionally promoted to approved rules.
- **Chapters 06–09:** editorially normalized from the detailed approved final contents preserved in the working conversation. Formatting and wording may differ from the original final Markdown drafts, while preserving approved principles, exceptions and hierarchy.
- **Annex A:** restructured from the separately approved, non-normative weekly-volume reference. Other appendices, research matrix, audit materials, Claude instructions and Python validator are **new supporting implementations**; they are not retroactively labeled approved HARDSET chapters.

## Key decisions preserved, not extrapolated

Ch01 high effort with safety and flexible RIR; Ch02 12-area Fixed Core, P/S/exposures and dynamic priorities; Ch03 two-set default with justified deviations; Ch04 criteria-first machine bias and safe failure access; Ch05 Lead-Set qualified progression and deload by need; Ch06 Goal-First Exposure-Aware and Baseline+Response; Ch07 Evidence-Limited Evaluation; Ch08 Continuous Progression + Optional Focus Blocks and Development/Maintenance intent; Ch09 build/validate with PASS/CONDITIONAL/FAIL.

## What the scientific verification did

The research appendix contains **23 sourced papers with DOI**, their relevant bounded conclusions, and an **exclusion log** for a retracted RIR study found during checking. This is a curated literature audit for HARDSET claims, **not a new exhaustive systematic review**. Other publications referenced in past discussion but not recoverable or verifiable are not fabricated.

## User-data boundary

The package intentionally contains **no individual historical training goals, biometrics, health history, performance records, calendar, personal training plan, location or identifying information**. Synthetic sample programs and exercise names are not inherited from any person. A new profile should be obtained in the destination chat.

## Compatibility guarantee

The Markdown chapters can be used as a canonical rule corpus. The Python validator is a **non-normative structural implementation prototype** and can be changed as long as the validated rule behavior stays aligned with the nine approved chapters and exceptions remain explicit. The complete master file is generated from component files; edit components and rebuild to prevent drift.
